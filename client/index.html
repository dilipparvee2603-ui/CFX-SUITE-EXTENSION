CUT TO F 3-WAY SPLIT

Splits selected layers into Head (2F) + Mid + Tail (2F), turns off Frame Blending on edges, and auto pre-composes.

Cut To F NON-DESTRUCTIVE

Automates precision 3-way split, edge Frame Blending bypass, and auto pre-composition:

1. Split Segments: First 2 frames (Head), Middle portion, and Last 2 frames (Tail).
2. Disable Frame Blending: Turned off on First 2F & Last 2F layers (Mid unchanged).
3. Auto Pre-Compose: Moves all attributes, adjusts comp duration to layer time span, and leaves new comp unopened.
• Source footage is never deleted or modified.
• Fully non-destructive: timing, layer order, and frame accuracy are 100% preserved.
• Multiple selected layers are processed independently into dedicated pre-comps.
